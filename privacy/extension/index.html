Privacy Policy

Zixia Flash Game Helper (browser extension) · Last updated: November 12, 2026 · Effective: November 12, 2026

1. Scope

This Privacy Policy applies to the Zixia Flash Game Helper browser extension for Google Chrome and Microsoft Edge, and to the optional Zixia native Flash component for Windows and Mac that the extension can use. The Zixia Browser apps have their own privacy policy.

In short: the extension and the native component do not collect your personal data, and do not send your browsing data, page content or cookies to the developer or to any third party. They have no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

2. What stays on your device

  • Settings: whether the native player is on, the game speed, the magnifier size and zoom, and the translation language are saved in your browser's extension storage.
  • Translation cache: translations already made are kept in the extension's local database (at most 20,000 entries) so the same text is not translated twice.
  • Game list cache: the list of recommended games shown in the popup is cached locally.
  • Native component: the component and its diagnostic log are files on your computer. They are not uploaded.

3. What the extension does on web pages

  • Flash content: the extension looks for Flash (SWF) content on the pages you open and plays it with the player bundled in the extension, or with the native component if you turn that on.
  • Game speed: the page's timers and clocks are changed only after you choose a speed other than 100% for that tab.
  • Translation: Chinese text on the page, on game canvases and in game text controls is read only while you have translation turned on. Editable fields and the values of input fields are never read. The text is translated on your device by your browser's built-in translator and is not uploaded; turning translation off restores the original text. Your browser may download language packs from its own vendor, under that vendor's privacy policy.
  • Magnifier: while you have the magnifier turned on for a tab, the extension takes snapshots of the visible part of that tab to show the area around the pointer enlarged. Snapshots are drawn into the lens and then discarded; they are never stored or sent anywhere.

4. Cookies and the native component

When you turn on the native player, it downloads the game's files itself. To keep the game signed in, the extension passes the cookies of the current game page to the native component installed on the same computer, and saves cookies that the game's server sets back to your browser. These cookies are used only in requests to the game's own servers. They are not sent to the developer or to anyone else.

5. Network requests the extension makes

  • Game files: the files a Flash game needs are requested from the websites that host the game, as the game itself would request them.
  • Recommended games: the popup reads a list of games from game.zixia.com. The request contains the interface language, the number of games wanted and the extension version. It contains no cookies and no personal information.
  • Native component version: the extension asks game.zixia.com for the latest version of the native component, so it can tell you when an update is available. The request contains the extension version only.
  • Local helper: Flash content that uses legacy TCP connections can use an optional helper program on your own computer. The extension connects to it at 127.0.0.1 only if you run it.

Like any web server, the servers that answer these requests can see basic technical information such as the IP address and the time of the request. Links to Zixia websites opened from the popup carry a tag saying they were opened from the extension, so the platform can count such visits. The websites and games you open have their own privacy practices.

6. Permissions and their purposes

  • Access to websites: web games are hosted on many different sites, often in nested frames, so the extension runs on the http and https pages you open in order to find and play Flash content and to provide game speed, translation and the magnifier there.
  • Cookies: used only as described in section 4.
  • Native messaging: used to talk to the optional native component on your computer.
  • Storage: used to save your settings and the caches described in section 2.
  • Network request rules: while a Flash game runs in a tab, a rule for that tab lets the bundled player read the game's files from other sites. The rule is removed when the tab closes.
  • Offscreen document, scripting and active tab: used to run the browser's built-in translator, to keep the browser from offering a second translation of a page being translated, and to take the magnifier's snapshot after you click the extension's icon.

7. Sharing, sale and use of data

We do not sell, share or transfer user data. The extension's use of information received through browser APIs follows the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements: such information is used only to provide the features described here, on your device.

8. Retention and deletion

Everything described in section 2 stays on your device until you remove it. Removing the extension from your browser deletes its settings and caches. The native component can be uninstalled from your system's list of installed apps on Windows, or by deleting the app on Mac.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the extension's features or legal requirements change. The latest version and its effective date are published on this page.

10. Contact us

For privacy questions or requests, contact us through game.zixia.com.
